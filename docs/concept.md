# Venturex — the concept, and the ventures on it (19 Oct 2026)

Written from the founder's claude.ai project "New Venture" (its concept doc of 3 Oct 2026, the
canvas "New Venture — service concept" of 9 Oct, and the founder's own words of 30 Sep and
18–19 Oct). The product's name is Venturex since 19 Oct 2026 (DECISIONS.md); the project, the
canvas and the handoff keep the working name as the literal title of a record.

## The product in one line

The door every idea goes through, and the platform that develops the ones that pass. Bring the
idea; get GO, PARKED or KILLED with the reason in writing; a GO opens a workspace that takes it,
stage by stage, to its first paying customer.

## The backbone

Two doors in, one path through, three ways out.

- **Your own idea**: a venture of the founder's.
- **Someone else's idea**: a customer, or an artist on the artist platform (Mainfloor).
- The same gates for both: five kill gates, one No kills. Survivors score out of 25 on five
  dimensions. The verdict is computed, never edited.
- **GO** at 18 or more: the venture is LIVE and the platform develops it through eight stages,
  each ending in a decision. The platform researches, drafts and builds; the founder decides and
  sells.
- **PARKED** at 13 to 17: a written revival trigger, an external event as a number with a
  deadline. When it fires, the idea is re-scored.
- **KILLED** below 13, or at a gate: the gate it failed, on record, so it cannot come back in
  eight months feeling fresh.

The eight stages: 1 clarify the customer problem · 2 research the market · 3 validate
willingness to pay · 4 define the offer · 5 minimum sellable product · 6 landing page or web
app · 7 launch marketing and sales · 8 document, automate, delegate. A GO lands at stage 3,
because the verdict covers 1 and 2.

The rules that do not bend: the gates come before the scoring and nobody argues with a gate;
below 18 is parked, not developed; every idea ends in exactly one state and nothing goes back on
the list; at most three ventures live and only one gets the hours; one test a week with a number
committed before it runs.

Venturex itself, by its own door: PARKED at 14/25 (3 Oct 2026), because no stranger has paid.
Its revival trigger is written: five of fifty strangers pre-pay within seven days, unrefunded on
day 21 (HANDOFF §1.3, §8 B8). The first build is therefore what the founder uses himself: the
free door and the workspace for his own ventures.
