## The gates, as the Gates board scripts them

1. Can you reach 10 potential buyers this week, without anyone's permission?  
   kills: kills: no reachable demand  
   on a No: No reachable demand side. Come back when you can name ten people you could message today.
2. Does someone already pay money for a worse version of this?  
   kills: kills: category creation  
   on a No: Category creation. 'Nobody does this yet' is usually a verdict, not a moat.
3. Can you ship something sellable in 30 days of solo work?  
   kills: kills: projects in disguise  
   on a No: A project disguised as a product. Cut it down to something sellable in 30 days, or park it.
4. Does it still earn if you aren't personally delivering each unit?  
   kills: kills: freelancing in disguise  
   on a No: Freelancing disguised as a business. Fine as income. Not a venture.
5. Will you still want to work on this in 18 months?  
   kills: kills: the week-three dip  
   on a No: The enthusiasm spike that dies in week three. Killed before it costs you anything.

## The score anchors, as the Score board scripts them

**Evidence of demand** (1 opinion · 5 someone paid)

1. My opinion, or a friend said "cool idea".
2. Search volume, forum complaints, a competitor exists.
3. Specific named people described the problem unprompted.
4. Someone asked you to build it, or asked what it would cost.
5. Someone has paid, pre-paid or signed something.

**Time to first euro** (1 over 90 days · 5 under 14)

1. More than 90 days from today.
2. Two to three months.
3. One to two months.
4. Two to four weeks.
5. Under 14 days, counted from today, not from "once it is built".

**Price point** (1 cheap and frequent · 5 high and recurring)

1. Cheap and frequent. Needs paid acquisition and scale before it pays.
2. Low ticket, one-off.
3. Mid ticket, one-off.
4. High ticket, or modest recurring.
5. High ticket and recurring.

**Distribution edge** (1 none yet · 5 buyers come to you)

1. "I would figure out marketing."
2. A channel you could learn.
3. A small audience, list or relationship you already hold.
4. An audience or relationship that reaches most buyers.
5. Buyers already come to you.

**Margin after fulfilment** (1 hours per unit · 5 near zero)

1. Every unit costs hours that do not compound. Physical or handmade.
2. Physical, with some leverage.
3. A service with templates behind it.
4. A productised service.
5. Digital. Fulfilment is close to zero.
