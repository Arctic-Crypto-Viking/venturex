# Sources and open items

Every page was read on 3 October 2026 unless a date is given. This tab says how the research was checked, lists what remains unverified, and gives the sources by section.

## How the research was done and checked

Eight research strands covered AI validators, co-founder and builder platforms, human-led programmes, planning and enterprise tools, free substitutes, demand-test tools, the research evidence, and market dynamics. They read vendor pages, pricing pages, papers and official sources on 3 October 2026.

Seven independent checks then re-opened 445 claims against their pages. 366 were confirmed, 18 of them with a caveat. 48 differed in a detail, 24 were not on the cited page, 5 were contradicted and 2 could not be reached. A final read-through compared the finished document with those tables, caught the corrections that had not carried over and tested the reasoning; its fixes are in this version.

The limits shape how far each claim can be trusted:

- The web-search allowance ran out part-way through every strand. The hunt for further 2026 entrants stopped early, so "no competitor was found" means "none among the products examined".
- Reddit could not be read at all, which is the largest hole in the sentiment evidence. Only a few Hacker News threads were readable.
- Several vendor pages were unreachable: Ideabrowser's pricing, Audos's site, Polsia's pricing and Kickstarter's live statistics.
- Page content came through a tool that summarises pages. The quotes were re-checked once, but spot-check any quote against the live page before reusing it in public.
- No accounts were created, no paid reports were bought and no idea was entered into any tool. What competitors' full reports contain is judged from sales pages and samples.

Not researched at all: data-protection rules for submitted ideas, the Digital Content Directive, national consumer-law differences, traffic estimates for any competitor, search volumes for validation queries, and European venture studios that co-build founders' own ideas.

The legal points in this document summarise public sources and are not legal advice.
